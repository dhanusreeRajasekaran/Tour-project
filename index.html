Welcome to Kerala Tourism

Welcome to the enchanting realm of Kerala, where the heart and essence of this divine land reside in its scenic landscapes and heartwarming villages, each weaving an interesting tale of timeless traditions and vibrant cultures.Known as "God's Own Country," Kerala is a mesmerizing coastal state on India’s southwestern tip, renowned for its tranquil backwaters, lush Western Ghats, pristine beaches, and rich cultural tapestry, offering an unparalleled blend of nature, adventure, and rejuvenation.This captivating destination seamlessly blends serene, palm-fringed backwaters and pristine emerald beaches with the mist-shrouded tea plantations of the Western Ghats. Visitors can experience tranquil houseboat cruises, rejuvenating Ayurvedic wellness therapies, vibrant Kathakali dance performances, and spicy, aromatic coastal cuisine. Known for its high quality of life, warm hospitality, and deep-rooted traditions, Kerala offers an enchanting, eco-conscious escape for travelers seeking both relaxation and adventure.

Kerala, a southwestern state along India's tropical Malabar Coast, is celebrated globally as "God's Own Country" due to its sweeping network of brackish lagoons, mist-laden hill stations, and rich cultural heritage. Locally and officially referred to as Keralam in Malayalam, the state's capital is Thiruvananthapuram.It's known for its palm-lined beaches and backwaters, a network of canals. Inland are the Western Ghats, mountains whose slopes support tea, coffee and spice plantations as well as wildlife. National parks like Eravikulam and Periyar, plus Wayanad and other sanctuaries, are home to elephants, langur monkeys and tigers.
